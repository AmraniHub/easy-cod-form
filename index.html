Getting started

1. Install the app and open Settings to choose the countries you deliver to, shipping prices, and quantity offers.

2. In your theme editor, add the COD Order Form block to a product page.

3. Turn on Shopify's Cash on Delivery manual payment method so shoppers can pay on delivery.

4. Place a test order — it appears in your Shopify Orders with payment pending.
