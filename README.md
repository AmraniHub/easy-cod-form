# Easy COD Form

**A cash-on-delivery order form for Shopify — published on the Shopify App Store.**

## What it does

Replaces Shopify's standard checkout with a single-step COD order form for markets where cash on delivery is the dominant payment method and card checkout kills conversion.

The shopper enters name, phone, and address, and confirms — no account, no payment step. Orders land in Shopify as normal orders, so fulfilment, inventory, and reporting keep working.

## Why COD needs its own form

In Morocco, the wider Maghreb, and much of MENA and South Asia, most e-commerce is COD. Standard checkout asks for payment details that the buyer will never provide, and the drop-off is severe. A COD-native form removes every field that doesn't serve the transaction.
